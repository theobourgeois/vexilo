<FeaturedSlot/>One slot. The first rule that matches wins.
1A contest is taking entriescontest.status = "open"
Why it's first: entries are the scarcest thing on the site and they expire.
  • Shows the theme, entry count and time left.
  • Three real entries are fanned out as the artwork, so it changes as people enter.
  • Logged out: "Enter" asks you to sign in, then drops you in the maker with the contest already picked.
2Voting is opencontest.status = "voting"
Votes need traffic. The home page is where the traffic is.
  • Shows how many of your 3 votes you've used, so you know there's something left to do.
  • When voting ends, the slot shows the winner for 7 days ("Winner: Port Aurel by @marisol_v").
3Nothing live: Flag of the dayfallback, always available
The slot is never empty. Flag of the day changes daily, so home always looks fresh.
  • The background color comes from the flag's stored palette (getStoredPalette), like flag pages already do. No image reads at render time.
  • When the slot is taken, Flag of the day moves down to a small card.
